Simple process. Managed operations.

We handle everything beyond checkout—so your team doesn’t take on new responsibilities.

From initial review to ongoing service, SmartPod operates as a fully managed amenity. Installation, stocking, monitoring, and support are coordinated end-to-end to ensure a consistent, reliable experience for your location.

1) Request placement

Submit venue details and preferred placement area. Our team reviews foot traffic, visibility, and use case to ensure the location is a strong fit.

2) Fit review

We confirm access, power availability, servicing path, and placement logistics to ensure clean installation and minimal disruption to your space.

3) Installation

Once approved, we schedule delivery and complete setup. Equipment is installed, tested, and ready for use— no staff training required.

4) Stocking & ongoing service

We manage product selection, restocking, and performance optimization. Inventory is adjusted over time based on demand, usage patterns, and location feedback.
